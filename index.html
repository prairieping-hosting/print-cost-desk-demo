What the calculation means

Material cost is grams divided by 1,000, multiplied by your material price per kilogram. Machine wear is print hours multiplied by your chosen hourly wear allowance. Labor is minutes divided by 60, multiplied by your labor rate. These are added together, then divided by one minus the target margin. The suggested price rounds up to the next cent.

A target margin is a share of the selling price, not a markup on cost. For a cost of 10 and a 20% margin, the suggested price is 12.50. Adding 20% to the cost gives 12.00 and a smaller margin.

Use one currency for all inputs. The demo displays USD as an example label and does not convert currencies. A result is a cost estimate; it does not show whether customers will pay that price.
